# The Mirror — 2–3 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

“AI can already find a stylist's portfolio, reviews, prices, and services. But those signals do not show how the stylist thinks when a real client brings uncertainty, constraints, and a request that may not describe the real need.”

Show The Mirror title and the product line: “A WebMCP experiment in making professional judgment machine-readable.”

## 0:20–0:40 — Why portfolio and reviews are insufficient

“A finished image shows the visual result. It does not reveal what the stylist protected, what they refused, which constraint became part of the design, or how they expected the work to evolve. Reviews can describe satisfaction, but not reconstruct the judgment chain.”

## 0:40–1:40 — Live WebMCP journey

Enter the visitor scenario:

> “I need to wear my hair up at work and can't look too dramatic, but outside work I don't want to feel boring. I also don't want frequent maintenance.”

Call `explore_stylist_fit`.

Point out that the result identifies designed visibility, identity expression, and low-maintenance judgment evidence. It surfaces `public-case-003` and `public-case-004` rather than prescribing a colour.

Call `get_work_details` for `public-case-003`.

Show the chain from context to design problem, judgment, design moves, and evolution. Emphasize the idea that public and private self-expression can be assigned to different visibility states.

## 1:40–2:15 — Consultation brief

Call `build_consultation_brief` using the visitor's work constraint, low maintenance preference, identity boundary, and reactions to the two cases.

“The output is not a formula or technical plan. It is a compact record of what the visitor is trying to achieve, what they want to avoid, and what still needs to be discussed in person.”

## 2:15–2:40 — Boundary

Show the uncertainty statement.

“The Mirror cannot verify actual hair integrity, chemical history, pigment buildup, or achievable lift from a remote conversation. Historical cases prove how SunSun has reasoned before; they do not prove that another person can reproduce the same result.”

## 2:40–3:00 — The Mirror thesis

“The Mirror is a small experiment with a larger question: when an agent helps someone discover a high-judgment professional, could compatibility with that professional's way of thinking become a new signal?”

Ending candidates for Supervisor selection:

> The Mirror doesn't choose your hair. It reveals how the stylist thinks.

> The Mirror makes professional judgment machine-readable.
