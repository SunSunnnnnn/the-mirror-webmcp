Professional judgment · WebMCP

How does the stylist think?

A WebMCP experiment in making professional judgment machine-readable—not remotely diagnosing hair or choosing a style for you.

The goal is not to help a visitor find a hairstyle. The goal is to help them understand whether they have found a hairstylist they can trust with the problem.

Four read-only tools

search_works

Search real evidence of how SunSun has reasoned about similar tensions and constraints.

get_work_details

Inspect the judgment chain behind one anonymized historical case.

explore_stylist_fit

Evaluate whether SunSun's way of thinking appears relevant to what the visitor needs.

build_consultation_brief

Turn the conversation into a non-persistent intent brief for an in-person consultation.

A different kind of answer

“I need to wear my hair up at work and can't look too dramatic, but outside work I don't want to feel boring. I also don't want frequent maintenance.”

The Mirror can find evidence about designed visibility, identity boundaries, and low-maintenance thinking.

It does not prescribe a colour. Actual hair condition, technical feasibility, and the final plan remain matters for in-person assessment.
