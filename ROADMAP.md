# The Mirror — Roadmap

All items below are **not part of the current MVP**.

## Track A — SunSun Consumer

Help agents understand SunSun's judgment evidence during professional discovery, improving trust and the quality of a later consultation.

## Track B — Professional Judgment Layer

Explore whether tacit professional judgment can become infrastructure that agents can query. Future test domains may include hairstylists, tattoo artists, photographers, interior designers, and other high-judgment professional services.

## Track C — Agentic Professional Discovery

Research whether agents can evaluate professionals using more than SEO, reviews, prices, and portfolios—adding **judgment compatibility** as a new discovery signal.
